PROGRAM 01 · 10 DAYS · 80 HOURS

AI/ML Training

Independent batch

Prepare data, train and evaluate ML models, forecast product demand and convert forecasts into inventory recommendations using explicit business rules.

HANDS-ON PROJECT

ML-Based Inventory Management

A retailer wants to understand which products may run short, which may have excess stock and how much to replenish.

10-day training planner

Project scope and deliverables
Project elementCoverage

ML outputExpected demand over a defined forecast horizon

ML estimates demand. Deterministic calculations and inventory policies produce replenishment recommendations.

The training addresses stockout-distorted sales, sparse demand and zero-demand metric cases. Forecast horizons and lead-time coverage must align; participants will not blindly extend a 7-day forecast to longer periods.

Learning outcomes and tools
  • Prepare and explore a retail dataset.
  • Create time-aware features without data leakage.
  • Build and compare basic regression and classification models.
  • Explain model performance and limitations.
  • Apply inventory rules to forecast outputs.
  • Deliver a reproducible inventory decision-support application.

Tools

Python, VS Code, NumPy, pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn, scikit-learn, joblib and Streamlit.

PROGRAM 02 · 10 DAYS · 80 HOURS

AI with Python

Independent batch

Develop Python applications, calculate pricing scenarios, connect to AI APIs and generate explainable pricing recommendations constrained by business policies.

HANDS-ON PROJECT

AI-Assisted Pricing and Margin Management

A retailer wants to decide whether to retain a price, offer a discount or review a price increase while protecting gross margins.

Core calculations

MeasureFormula
Gross profit per unitSelling price − Unit cost
Gross margin %(Selling price − Unit cost) ÷ Selling price × 100
Markup %(Selling price − Unit cost) ÷ Unit cost × 100
Price after discountCurrent price × (1 − Discount fraction)
Minimum selling priceUnit cost ÷ (1 − Minimum gross-margin fraction)

Input checks include invalid costs, zero prices and invalid margin targets. Calculations use a consistent tax basis and an agreed cost definition.

Python calculates prices and margins and enforces constraints. The LLM explains results and recommends from permitted options. Any assumed change in sales volume is explicitly identified as a scenario assumption.

Learning outcomes and tools
  • Build a structured Python application using files and APIs.
  • Calculate prices, discounts, gross profit and gross margins correctly.
  • Compare pricing options under defined constraints.
  • Integrate an LLM with structured and validated responses.
  • Retrieve relevant policy content and cite supporting sources.
  • Deliver a pricing recommendation application with human review.

Tools

Python, VS Code, pandas, NumPy, visualisation libraries, scikit-learn for introductory exercises, an agreed LLM/embedding API, a lightweight vector index and Streamlit.

Training materials and preparation

Training materials and submissions

Each batch receives prepared datasets, starter code, lab exercises, capstone instructions and assessment criteria. Participants submit their completed project and documentation.

Preparation before delivery

  • Configure Python, the development environment and required packages.
  • Provide prepared retail datasets and sample business policies.
  • Confirm internet access and approved LLM/embedding API access for Program 2.
  • Agree API usage budgets and use approved training data.
  • Verify the classroom setup and run a short prerequisite assessment.
Scope and delivery boundary

Both programs target foundation-level workplace capability through guided projects. Advanced deep learning, causal price optimisation, production MLOps, advanced RAG and autonomous agents are outside the core scope.

Projects use prepared training data and simulated recommendations, with human review before business action. Program 2 receives its own inventory inputs and runs independently of Program 1.

Delivery sequence: Program 1 AI/ML Training, followed by Program 2 AI with Python. Each program runs for 10 days, giving 20 training days and 160 scheduled hours in total.
